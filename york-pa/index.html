Why this matters in Pennsylvania

Three deadlines already on your desk

For Pennsylvania organizations, security stopped being optional — it's now written into state law, insurance renewals, and grant applications.

The amended PA breach law

Since September 2024, Pennsylvania's Breach Notification Act requires notifying the PA Attorney General for breaches affecting 500+ residents, providing 12 months of credit monitoring for SSN/financial breaches — and unauthorized access now counts as a breach, not just theft.

Cyber-insurance questionnaires

Renewals now ask hard questions about MFA, backups, endpoint protection, and incident response. Wrong answers mean denied applications or higher premiums. We help York organizations answer honestly — and close the gaps behind the answers.

Grant readiness for nonprofits

Federal programs like the DHS Nonprofit Security Grant Program — and a growing number of private funders — want a documented vulnerability assessment before they write the check. Our plain-English assessment is built to be the one funders accept.
