Why now

Three reasons Central PA organizations are acting

The stakes changed — and the same steps that protect you also keep you compliant.

A new PA law raised the stakes

Pennsylvania's updated Breach Notification Act (Act 33 of 2024) requires notifying the Attorney General for larger breaches and providing 12 months of free credit monitoring. A breach in PA just got more expensive.

Cyber-insurance got strict

Renewals now demand proof of MFA, backups, and an incident-response plan — nearly half of 2025 claims traced back to logins without MFA. We help you pass the questionnaire honestly, and often lower the premium.

Funders want a security assessment

Security has quietly become a grant requirement. We produce the documented, plain-English assessment funders accept.
